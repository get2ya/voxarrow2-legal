1. Who we are

AISoftCrafters Inc. ("we", "us") publishes the mobile puzzle game Voxarrow 2 (the "App"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, why, who receives it, and what choices you have. Contact: works@aisoftcrafters.com.

In short. We run no server of our own and the App has no account system. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other directly identifying information. Your game progress stays on your device. The App uses three Google services — Google AdMob (ads), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Google Play Games Services (optional leaderboards) — and those services collect the limited data described below.

2. Information stored only on your device

Game progress (levels cleared, stars, records, coins, items, daily puzzle and attendance status, sound/vibration settings) is saved in the App's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Google signals, age and gender reports

We have turned on Google signals in Google Analytics. For users who are signed in to a Google account and have allowed ads personalization in their Google account, Google may associate the Analytics data with that account to give us aggregated, anonymous reports of estimated age range, gender and interests. We only see aggregated statistics, never information about an individual user, and Google hides small groups. We do not set a user ID in Analytics and we never send your name, email address or any text you type. You can stop this association at any time by turning off ads personalization at My Ad Center or managing your Google account activity at myaccount.google.com.

5. Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

Leaderboards are optional; every other part of the game works without them. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your player ID, gamer tag and avatar are shared with the App and the scores you submit are shown on the leaderboard next to your gamer tag. Google Play Games may sign you in automatically when you have previously chosen to do so in the Play Games app. You decide who can see your profile (Everyone, Friends only, Only you) in your Google Play Games profile settings. We never receive your email address or real name.

6. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required)

Where the law requires it, the App shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform when it starts. Until that consent step has completed, the App requests no ads and Google Analytics collection stays switched off. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from the App's Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown in regions where a choice applies).

7. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (path varies by device).
  • Ad personalization: My Ad Center.
  • Leaderboard data: Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, then choose this game; or myaccount.google.com.
  • Game progress: uninstall the App, or Settings → Apps → Voxarrow 2 → Storage → Clear data.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep no personal information on servers of our own. Analytics event data is kept in Google Analytics for the retention period set in our Analytics property (no longer than 14 months for user-level data) and then deleted automatically; aggregated reports may be kept longer. Advertising and Play Games data are kept by Google under Google's policies. To ask us to delete Analytics data linked to your device's app-instance ID, email works@aisoftcrafters.com; we reply within 30 days.

9. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by Google LLC as described above and is not shared with any other advertising partner. We may disclose information if required by law.

10. International transfers

Google may process the data described above on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards in Google's privacy policy (including standard contractual clauses where applicable).

11. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS). Data on your device is kept in the App's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Children

The App is a general-audience game that is not directed to children under 13, and it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. Ads are limited to content rated "G". We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or the Korean Personal Information Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, to object, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the email above, use the controls in section 7, or contact Google directly. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes are announced in the App's update notes or on this page before they take effect.

15. Contact

AISoftCrafters Inc. — works@aisoftcrafters.com

제4조 (Google 신호 및 연령·성별 통계)

개발자는 Google Analytics의 Google 신호 기능을 사용합니다. Google 계정에 로그인되어 있고 Google 계정에서 광고 개인 최적화를 허용한 이용자의 경우, Google이 분석 데이터를 해당 계정과 연결하여 추정 연령대·성별·관심사에 관한 익명 집계 보고서를 개발자에게 제공할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 개별 이용자에 관한 정보가 아닌 집계 통계만 볼 수 있으며, 인원이 적은 항목은 Google이 숨깁니다. 개발자는 분석에 사용자 ID를 설정하지 않으며 이름·이메일·입력 텍스트를 전송하지 않습니다. 내 광고 센터에서 광고 개인 최적화를 끄거나 Google 계정에서 활동 설정을 변경하면 이 연결을 중단할 수 있습니다.

제5조 (리더보드 — Google Play 게임즈 서비스)

리더보드는 선택 기능이며 이용하지 않아도 게임의 다른 모든 기능을 사용할 수 있습니다. Google Play 게임즈에 로그인하면 플레이어 ID·게이머 태그·아바타가 본 앱에 제공되고, 제출한 점수가 게이머 태그와 함께 리더보드에 표시됩니다. 이용자가 Play 게임즈 앱에서 자동 로그인을 허용한 경우 자동으로 로그인될 수 있습니다. 프로필 공개 범위(모든 사용자·친구만·나만)는 Google Play 게임즈 프로필 설정에서 정할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 이메일 주소나 실명을 제공받지 않습니다.

제6조 (동의 — 유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 등)

법령상 필요한 지역에서는 앱 실행 시 Google 사용자 메시지 플랫폼의 동의 화면이 표시됩니다. 이 동의 절차가 끝나기 전에는 광고를 요청하지 않으며 Google Analytics 수집도 꺼져 있습니다. 맞춤형 광고에 동의하지 않아도 비맞춤형 또는 제한된 광고가 표시될 수 있습니다. 앱의 설정 → 광고 개인정보 설정(해당 지역에서 표시)에서 언제든지 선택을 확인·변경할 수 있습니다.

제7조 (이용자의 선택권과 행사 방법)

  • 광고 ID: 기기 설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고에서 재설정·삭제(기기마다 경로가 다를 수 있음)
  • 광고 개인 최적화: 내 광고 센터
  • 리더보드 데이터: Google Play 게임즈 앱 → 프로필 → 설정 → Play 게임즈 계정 및 데이터 삭제에서 이 게임 선택, 또는 myaccount.google.com

개발자는 자체 서버에 개인정보를 보관하지 않습니다. 분석 데이터는 제3조의 기간이 지나면 Google Analytics에서 자동 삭제되며, 광고·Play 게임즈 데이터는 Google 정책에 따라 보관·파기됩니다. 기기의 앱 인스턴스 ID에 연결된 분석 데이터 삭제를 원하시면 works@aisoftcrafters.com으로 요청해 주십시오. 30일 이내에 회신합니다.

Google SDK가 전송하는 데이터는 전송 구간에서 TLS/HTTPS로 암호화됩니다. 기기 내 정보는 앱 전용 저장 영역에 보관됩니다. 다만 완전히 안전한 전송·저장 방식은 존재하지 않습니다.
